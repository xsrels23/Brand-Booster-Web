Objective 3

Teacher & Staff Spotlights

Brief videos introducing staff members, their roles, and why they love teaching. This humanizes your brand and builds trust by showing the mentors who will be caring for the children.

MANDATORY FILMING STANDARD

  • • Audio Setup: Sit in a quiet room. Hold the phone or microphone about 4 inches from your mouth.
  • • Statement Shot: About 25 seconds, vertical 9:16. Read your statement below to the lens, framed from the chest up with a clean classroom background.
  • • B-Roll Shots: Film 2 steady clips (6–7 seconds each) of the teacher with students or preparing materials.
  • • File Names: Statement: Obj-3_Shot_1 · B-roll: Obj-3_Shot_2, Obj-3_Shot_3.
  • • Orientation: Hold your phone vertically (9:16). No talking needed: music replaces the sound.
  • • The Cast: 4 staff in two pairs. Staff 1 & 4 are one pair, Staff 2 & 3 the other (match the name banners below).
  • • Action Shots 1, 2, 3, 5, 6: Real moments: arrival (Staff 1 & 4), active play (Staff 1), homework help (Staff 2 & 3), a hands-on experiment (Staff 2 & 3), dismissal (Staff 4). Hold steady for 6–8 seconds each.
  • • Intro Shots 4 & 7: Each pair turns to the camera with a big wave and smile (Shot 4: Staff 2 & 3; Shot 7: Staff 1 & 4). Do it 2–3 times so we catch the best one.
  • • Group Finale, Shot 8: All four staff together with students, posing for the camera.
  • • File Names: Obj-3_Shot_1 to Obj-3_Shot_8, in the order above.

  • • Demo Clips: One clip of the finished result (for example the tidy desk with the timer running) and one of a child doing the tip, 6–7 seconds each. For the voiceover format, add 2 more demo angles.
  • • File Names: Tip: Obj-10_Shot_1 · Result: Obj-10_Shot_2 · Child: Obj-10_Shot_3 · Voiceover format only: Obj-10_Shot_4, Obj-10_Shot_5.
